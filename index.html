How Bacteria & Viruses Cause Disease

GCSE-level interactive simulation: toxins vs hijacking

What you’re about to see

Bacteria (living cells)

  • They reproduce on their own.
  • Some make toxins that damage tissues and cause symptoms.
  • In the simulation: toxins reduce “cell health”.

Viruses (not cells)

  • They must get inside a cell to reproduce.
  • They hijack the cell’s machinery to make more viruses.
  • In the simulation: viral load rises until the cell bursts.

These are simplified models to explain core ideas (GCSE). Real infections are more complex.

Bacteria: toxin production

Bacteria can release toxins. Toxins can damage cells and trigger symptoms (e.g., inflammation, diarrhoea, fever). In this model, toxin particles spread and reduce the “cell health” bar.

Running
